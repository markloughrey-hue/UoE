How the CRED Score is built

Retrospective donor patterns → forward‑looking lens across your whole database
→
01

Match & enrich

Your first‑party records (name, email, graduation year, course) are matched to CRED's graph — 900M+ executive profiles, 70M+ companies. ~85%+ match rate, no data retained.

→
02

Learn from past donors

We model your last 5–10 years of giving to find the traits that actually predict a gift — founding age, sector, city, role tenure, student affinity.

→
03

Score the database

That lens is applied to every alum from the day they graduate — a live 1–10 score, re‑calculated as careers move and liquidity events approach.

04

Trigger engagement

Job changes, board seats, fundraising rounds and pre‑IPO signals push alumni up the list automatically — so you engage three years early, not three years late.

The score sharpens the moment your internal data goes in

This sample runs on CRED's data alone. When Exeter's giving history and engagement records are matched in, the model learns what makes an Exeter alum give — and the predictive accuracy steps up materially. Fully GDPR & SOC 2 compliant; your data is matched and returned, never retained.
